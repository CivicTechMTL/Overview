# Code of Conduct

Civic Tech Montréal is a community where everyone should feel welcome, safe
and able to take part — at meetups, online, in Slack and on GitHub.

## Our commitment
We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation,
disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, technical
skill level or economic standing. We do not tolerate harassment in any form.

## What we expect
- **Be welcoming.** Introduce yourself, include newcomers, and remember
  everyone was new once.
- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- **Make room.** Share the floor, listen, and invite quieter voices in.
- **Share and respect pronouns.** When you introduce yourself, share your
  pronouns if you're comfortable (for example, "I'm Sam, she/her"). If you're
  not sure of someone's pronouns, ask or use their name. Always use the
  pronouns people tell you. If you make a mistake, correct yourself briefly
  and move on.
- **Respect language choices.** People may take part in English or French.
- **Respect people's lived experience.** The people most affected by a
  problem should help shape the response.

## What's not OK
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Deliberately using the wrong pronouns or name for someone
- Unwanted physical contact or sexual attention
- Sharing someone's personal information without their permission
- Repeatedly disrupting conversations, events or discussions

## What happens if someone breaks these guidelines
Organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including a warning or
removal from events and online spaces.

## Raising a concern
If you experience or witness harassment, or have any other concern,
please talk to an organizer.

## Where this applies
Meetups and events, Slack, GitHub, and any other Civic Tech Montréal space.
