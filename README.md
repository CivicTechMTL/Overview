# Civic Tech Montréal

**English** · [Français](README.fr.md)

We're a bilingual, volunteer community of residents, technologists, designers
and anyone curious about how tools and data can strengthen civic life in
Montréal. No experience required — just curiosity.

**Our mission:** to build technology with community, for community.

**Our vision:** a future where Civic Tech Montréal is a vibrant and diverse
community garden that maximizes advocacy, accessibility, and agency to build
in Montréal.

## Find your way in

**1. Open door — come as you are**
Your first step is simple: come to a meetup. Check [Luma](https://luma.com/civictechmontreal)
for the next one, or say hi on [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/civictechmontreal/shared_invite/zt-3sx2os89v-6rIwr2sNmhl6r2FZvyPoCQ).

**2. Learn by doing**
Join a workshop, skill-share or build day and get hands-on with civic tech.
New to the terms? Start with our [Civic Tech Glossary](https://github.com/CivicTechMTL/Organizing/blob/main/Resources/Civic%20Tech%20Glossary.md).

**3. Problem to project**
Bring a civic problem you care about, or help build a solution to one.
We're looking for problem partners, not just builders.

A project is an ongoing civic tech initiative, built in the open, to learn and
to help our community. Projects are open to anyone: you can pitch your own or
join one that already exists. Most projects move through four stages:

> **Pitch** the problem or idea → **Recruit** collaborators → **Design** (explore and iterate) → **Build**

To pitch at a meetup, you get one minute to answer three questions:
What's the project? Who can help? What are you working on tonight?

[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose), post in Slack, or bring your idea to a meetup.

Current projects:
- [Brique par Brique](https://github.com/CivicTechMTL/BriqueParBriqueData)

**4. Connect and sustain**
Help run the community. At each meetup we need people to:
- emcee the evening
- greet people at the door
- find speakers, or speak about civic tech work you're doing

Join the **#organizing** channel on Slack to pitch in, browse
[volunteer roles](https://github.com/CivicTechMTL/Organizing/tree/main/Roles)
and read our [contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Who can contribute?

Everybody. Don't code? No problem. Civic tech needs researchers, designers,
data analysts, developers, artists, project managers, strategists, legal and
policy folks, community organizers, and residents who know their
neighbourhood. You can also help by building relationships with community
groups or government, doing communications or user research, or simply
spreading the word.

## What happens at a meetup?

We meet monthly at the Cité-des-Hospitalières. A typical evening looks like this:
1. **Socialize** and introductions
2. **Speaker** on a civic tech topic
3. **Project pitches**, old and new
4. **Break into groups** and work on projects

We also run other events, like build nights, showcases, hackathons and
bootcamps. Dates are always on [Luma](https://luma.com/civictechmontreal).
